
LOST OR STOLEN BANK CARD — CUSTOMER GUIDANCE

Purpose:
Provide general safety guidance when a customer reports a lost
or stolen debit, credit, or ATM card.

IMMEDIATE ACTIONS

1. Contact the bank immediately through its official mobile app,
   website, verified customer-care number, or branch.

2. Request that the lost or stolen card be blocked to prevent
   unauthorized use.

3. Ask the bank for a complaint or service-request reference number.

4. Review recent account and card transactions for suspicious
   or unauthorized activity.

5. If any unauthorized transaction is identified, report it
   immediately through the bank's official dispute or fraud
   reporting process.

6. Request a replacement card using official banking channels.

7. Keep relevant transaction records, messages, screenshots,
   and communication reference numbers.

SECURITY PRECAUTIONS

- Never share your OTP, PIN, CVV, or banking password.
- Do not follow suspicious links or call unverified phone numbers.
- Use only official banking channels.

IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

A lost or stolen card does not automatically mean that an
unauthorized transaction has occurred.

If the customer reports only card theft, prioritize securing
the card and reviewing account activity.

If the customer also reports unauthorized transactions,
provide appropriate fraud-reporting and dispute guidance.

LIMITATIONS

This document provides general customer safety guidance.
It does not establish bank-specific obligations, compensation
eligibility, refund deadlines, or internal compliance procedures.
Those details must be verified against current official policies.
